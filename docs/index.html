Bark Cam

A half-dollar-size camera board that listens for your dog. When it hears a bark, it snaps a photo and texts it to you on Telegram — no cloud, no AI model, just signal processing on the board.

New in v1.1 — auto-exposure for changing light, a few photos per barking episode, and a quiet-hours schedule.

Chrome or Edge · no install · your saved settings are kept

Setup

Two minutes, one time

After flashing, the board walks you through it. Everything is saved on the board — no account, no cloud.

  1. 01
    Power the board (USB). For 10 minutes it broadcasts an open network barkcam-config — connect your phone to it.
  2. 02
    Open barkcam.local in your phone's browser (or 192.168.4.1 if that doesn't resolve).
  3. 03
    Enter your home WiFi, a Telegram bot token (from @BotFather) and your user ID (from @userinfobot). Hit Save settings. Done — the board closes its access point when your phone leaves.
Other ways

Prefer the terminal?

  • Download the firmware and flash with esptool: 
    $ esptool.py --chip esp32s3 write_flash 0x0 barkcam-v1.1.bin
  • Build from source (PlatformIO) — the repo has full docs.
  • Safari and Firefox don't support WebSerial — use the download above, or open this page in Chrome/Edge.
